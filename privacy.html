Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

rclone-backup is used only by its owner (otccrypta@gmail.com) to back up the owner's own files.

  • Data accessed: only files and folders that rclone-backup itself created in the owner's Google Drive (Google scope drive.file). It cannot see any other Drive files.
  • Use: storing and restoring encrypted backup copies. The backup is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Storage: backup data stays in the owner's Google Drive. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact: otccrypta@gmail.com
